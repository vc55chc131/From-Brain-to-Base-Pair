## Scientific boundary

The audit concerns sample-center coordinates, the selected seed centers, and explicitly specified matching rules. It does not establish physical tissue overlap, validate anatomical identities of nearby samples, or show that every AHBA ROI approach is infeasible. The absence of eligible pairs is not evidence of molecular equivalence. It prevents the proposed paired inference under the audited settings.

The source enrichment chart was also given a color-only correction for the author's files. That correction preserves positions and labels but does not validate its probabilities. The chart is excluded from the clean scientific manuscript.
